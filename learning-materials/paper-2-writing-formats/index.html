Paper 2 - Writing Formats

HKDSE English Language · genre bank

Formats
  • Letter to the Editor — public argument or complaint to a newspaper / magazine editor
  • Article — magazine or school-publication piece to inform, discuss, or entertain a wide readership
  • Proposal — suggest a plan or project to a decision-maker (principal, committee, NGO)
  • Report — present findings / situation / recommendations in a clear, factual layout
  • Speech — spoken-style address to a live audience (assembly, ceremony, competition)

Other Part A genres may appear (email, blog, questionnaire). Always follow the task’s stated format.
