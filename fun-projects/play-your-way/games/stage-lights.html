How to play
  1. Play a stretch of notes first, like a usual music game — hit lane A, B or C as each note crosses the white line (tap the lane or card, or press 1 / 2 / 3).
  2. A lyric, setlist line, or cue card then appears while the song keeps going. Keep hitting notes as you read it.
  3. Answer on the special notes — one in each lane for A, B and C. Only the correct special note scores as the answer.
  4. Perfect and Good keep the combo. A miss or a wrong special note costs a mic. No mics left and the crowd goes quiet.
