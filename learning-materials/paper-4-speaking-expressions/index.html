Paper 4 - Speaking Expressions

Beginning a Discussion

Greeting others and starting the discussion

  • Hello/Good morning/Good afternoon, everyone.
  • Let’s begin our discussion, shall we?
  • Okay, let’s begin.

Defining the task

  • We’re here today to talk about …
  • We need to decide/find out …
  • Let’s discuss what to include in …

Stating your first point

  • Let’s begin with/by discussing …
  • The first thing we have to talk about is …
  • First of all/To begin, …

Asking for opinions

  • What’s your opinion?
  • What do you think of this?
  • Does anyone have any ideas/suggestions?
Suggestions

Inviting suggestions

  • Are there any (other) suggestions?
  • What do you think?
  • What would you suggest?
  • Do you think that’s a good idea?

Putting forward suggestions

  • Let’s see if we can …
  • I suggest/think (that) …
  • Perhaps we could/should …
  • Why don’t we …?
  • Shall/Should we …?
  • How/What about …?

Giving reasons

  • I say this because …
  • … because/since/as …
  • This is because …
  • My reason(s) is/are …
  • … due to …
  • … thanks to …

Giving details and examples

  • Furthermore/Moreover, …
  • In addition to/On top of that, …
  • Here’s an example.
  • An example (of what I’ve said) is …
  • For example/instance, …
  • … such as/like/including …
Agreeing and Disagreeing

When you agree

  • I agree (with you).
  • I think so too.
  • Yes, you’re right.
  • I’d go along with that.
  • I support your view.
  • I feel the same.

When you strongly agree

  • I totally/completely agree with you.
  • I’m strongly in favour of this.
  • Absolutely/Exactly/Indeed!
  • I couldn’t agree with you more.

Disagreeing

  • I’m not so sure about that.
  • I totally disagree with that idea/suggestion.
  • I don’t agree/think so.
  • I’m afraid I disagree.
  • I (highly) doubt that.

Offering alternatives

  • Shouldn’t we consider … instead?
  • I think quite the opposite.
  • Instead of …, why not …?
  • Another approach to that is …
  • On the other hand/On the contrary …
  • Wouldn’t it be better to …?
Asking for Clarification

Stating the need for clarification

  • I’m not sure what you mean.
  • I’m afraid I didn’t catch that.
  • I didn’t quite get what you said.
  • I don’t quite understand.

Asking for repetition

  • Could you say that again?
  • Can you repeat what you’ve just said?
  • Would you mind saying that again?

Asking for further information

  • Could you explain …?
  • Can you give an example?
  • What do you mean by …?
  • Why do you think so?
  • Could you elaborate on that?
  • Would you mind telling us a little more about that?
Pros, Cons and Preferences

Discussing pros and cons

  • Let’s discuss the pros and cons of …
  • What do you think are the advantages and disadvantages of …?
  • Let’s explore the other side of the issue.
  • Although … , there are some benefits/drawbacks to …

Talking about preferences

  • I support …
  • I think/believe … is better because …
  • I like … more than …
  • I choose/prefer … because …
  • My choice/preference would be …
  • Although … is a good choice, I still think …
Resolving Disagreements

Suggesting a compromise

  • Let’s come to a compromise.
  • Why don’t we agree to disagree?
  • Shall we agree on a compromise?

Acknowledging both parties

  • I think both of you make good points.
  • Let’s combine both suggestions.
  • You have both made good suggestions.
  • I think we could combine your ideas by …

Making a decision later

  • How about we make a decision later?
  • Shall we discuss these ideas later? Let’s move on to …
  • Perhaps we can come back to this later.
Summing Up

Seeking agreement

  • Shall I sum up?
  • Let’s conclude our discussion, shall we?
  • If no one has anything else to add, let’s sum up our discussion.

Going into the summary

  • In short, we all agreed that …
  • To sum up, we have decided to …
  • To conclude, we have mentioned that …

Inviting others to add to the summary

  • Do you have anything to add?
  • Did I miss anything in my summary?
Beginning and Organising a Response

Beginning a response

  • I’d choose/say/prefer …
  • From my perspective, …
  • In my opinion/experience, …
  • Based on my understanding, …
  • Personally speaking, …
  • If I were (someone)/in that situation, I would …
