How to play
  1. Each scene is a new clue, note, or interrogation.
  2. Tap lead A, B or C (or press 1 / 2 / 3).
  3. Press Follow this lead (or Enter) to pin it to the case board.
  4. A right clue builds the case. A wrong one, or waiting too long, costs a notebook page. No pages left and the case goes cold.
