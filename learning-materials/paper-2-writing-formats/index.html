Paper 2 - Writing Formats

HKDSE English Language · genre bank

Formats
Letters & Emails
Letter

Letter to the Editor

Formal view on a public issue — thesis last in opening; Yours faithfully / sincerely.

Open → Formal letter

Formal Letter / Email

Write to one reader or organisation — request, complain, apply; same body, different layout.

Open →
Magazine & Web
Article

Article

Newspaper / magazine / school magazine — hook, angle, no letter sign-off.

Open → Blog

Blog Entry

Share experience or tips — hook, angle, conversational; no Dear… / Yours…

Open → Review

Review

Film / book / product — overall judgement, strengths and weaknesses with reasons.

Open →
School Writing
Proposal

Proposal

Suggest a plan — aims, details, resources, benefits; ask for approval.

Open → Report

Report

Facts under headings — focus line (not debate thesis); findings → recommendations.

Open → Questionnaire

Questionnaire

Complete every prompt in order — short, relevant answers; not one long essay.

Open → Speech

Speech

Write for the ear — greet, thesis, signposted points, thank the audience.

Open →
Essay & Story
Essay

Essay

Argue or discuss a view with reasons — thesis, points, short counter-view; no letter sign-off.

Open → Story

Story

Narrate a clear event — usually past tense; use any given first or last line exactly.
