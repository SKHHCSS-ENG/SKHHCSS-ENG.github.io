❓ How to play · 玩法說明
Pick your Performer & Soundtrack difficulty in Setlist & soundcheck — Night Set (★ Easy), Pulse (★★ Normal · Hold Notes), or Neon (★★★ Hard · Double + Hold Notes).
  1. Play a stretch of notes first — hit lane A, B or C as each prism bar crosses the line. Hold down on long glowing ribbons, and press both lanes on double notes!
  2. Hit 6 notes in a row to ignite 🔥 ON FIRE! mode for 2× note points and a blazing stage transformation.
  3. A lyric, setlist line, or cue card appears during Read Time while normal notes keep coming. When time is up, answer on the 3 golden special notes (A, B, C).
  4. Perfect and Good keep the combo. A miss or wrong special note costs a mic. In 2P Split Screen, first correct answer wins a +25 speed bonus!
