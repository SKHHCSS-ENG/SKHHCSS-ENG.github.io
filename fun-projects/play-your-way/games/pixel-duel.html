How to play
  1. Read the question, then slash rune A, B or C — tap the orb or card, or press 1 / 2 / 3.
  2. Correct: the boss is stunned. Keep slashing, or tap the boss, for a Break combo.
  3. Wrong or too slow: the boss attacks. Jump the shockwaves, or slash to parry them.
  4. Use Boost (Shift) for a short power dash. If all shields break, the duel ends.
