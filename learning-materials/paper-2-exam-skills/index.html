Paper 2 - Exam Skills

HKDSE English Language · paper map, writing skills and exam habits

Paper Map

Weighting & time

  • Paper 2 Writing = 25% of the subject
  • 2 hours for Parts A and B together

Part A (Compulsory) — 10%

  • Everyone sits Part A — one short, guided task (~200 words)
  • Cover the given prompts; genres vary (article, email, blog…) — read the task carefully

Part B — choose 1 of 4 questions — 15%

  • Longer task (~400 words); from 2024: 4 questions (was 8)
  • Choose by topic + genre you know — not by “looks easy”
  • Mark your choice with an X in the Question-Answer Book (question-number box)

How you are marked

  • Content — task fulfilled; relevant, developed ideas; audience awareness
  • Language — accurate grammar; varied sentences; vocabulary and tone fit the genre
  • Organisation — clear structure; logical paragraphs; cohesion for the text type

Timing

  • One clock for A + B — leave a clear block for Part B
  • Classroom guide (not HKEAA): ~40 min A · ~70–75 min B · leftover for plan / check — plan on the rough-work sheet only
Writing Skills

Purpose · Audience · Tone

  • Purpose — what must this text do? (inform, persuade, propose, complain…)
  • Audience — who reads it? (editor / public, principal, classmates…)
  • Tone / register — formal, semi-formal, or friendly? Stay consistent; match genre features (greeting, headline, sign-off)
Example

Letter to the SCMP about MTR crowding → persuade + complain; editor / public; formal, calm, evidence-based — not chatty WhatsApp.

Task Fulfilment

  • Underline every requirement (role, reader, points, word guide) — tick them off as you draft
  • Stay on the asked topic; missing a bullet costs Content marks
  • Part A: use the given information; Part B: develop your own ideas with enough support
Example

Part A asks for three Open Day improvements → three clear, developed points, not one long story.

Planning

  • 3–5 minutes: purpose / audience / 3–4 main points before writing
  • Decide paragraph order; for Part B, pick the question you can finish well

Paragraphing

  • One clear idea per paragraph; topic sentence when it helps
  • Short intro → developed body → ending that fits the genre — not one giant block

Cohesion

  • Precise connectives (however, therefore, in addition) — not and / and / and
  • Pronouns and synonyms for flow; keep tense and viewpoint steady unless the genre shifts

Language for Marks

  • Clear over showy — HKEAA warns against over-ambitious language that confuses
  • Mix simple and complex sentences; exact topic words beat rare words used wrongly
  • Spelling and punctuation matter when a slip changes meaning
Exam Habits

Part B choice

  • Choose by topic and genre you practise — not panic-picking in the room

Before you write

  • A few minutes on ideas + text type + audience before you write

Clarity

  • Clarity first — no rare words or ultra-long sentences that hide your meaning

Stay on task

  • Every paragraph should earn Content marks

Timing

  • Manage the 2 hours: finish both parts with time to re-read for missing bullets and slips

Answer book

  • Write on every line where you can; plan on the rough-work sheet, then a clean answer in the book
  • Part B: put an X next to the question you chose

Word guides

  • ~200 / ~400 are targets for a full sample — no automatic deduction for going over; empty padding does not help

Final check

  • Leave 3–5 minutes: required points? Genre features? Tone? Obvious grammar slips?
