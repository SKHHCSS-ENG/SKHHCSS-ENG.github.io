How to play
  1. A customer arrives with a ticket — that is your English question.
  2. Tap the dish that matches answer A, B or C (or press 1 / 2 / 3).
  3. Press Serve (or Enter) before the patience bar runs out.
  4. Correct orders earn coins and combos. Wrong dishes or walk-outs cost a heart. Empty hearts end the shift.
