True / False / Not Given

Match, contradict, or go beyond — stay inside the passage.

True / False / Not Given

What It Asks

  • True (T) — the claim matches the text (same meaning OK)
  • False (F) — the claim contradicts the text
  • Not Given (NG) — the text does not settle the claim
  • Use the passage only — no outside knowledge

How To Decide

  • Find the relevant lines first
  • Check numbers, names, and absolute words (always / never / all / only)
  • Same meaning = T (synonyms OK)
  • Silent or only related → NG

Common Traps

  • One wrong detail (date, number, person, place)
  • True in real life, not in the text → NG
  • Cause, time, or who reversed
  • Stronger / weaker than the text (must vs may; all vs some)

Mini Example

Example

Passage
Our school library opens at 7:45 a.m. on weekdays. Form 4 students may borrow up to four books for two weeks. Magazines stay in the reading corner and cannot be taken home.

# Statement Answer
1 Form 4 students may borrow four books for two weeks. T — stated (same meaning)
2 Magazines can be taken home for one week. F — text says cannot be taken home
3 The library opens earlier on exam days. NG — exam days not mentioned
