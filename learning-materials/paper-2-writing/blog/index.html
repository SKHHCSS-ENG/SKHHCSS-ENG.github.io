P2 / P3B

Blog Entry

Share experience, opinion, or tips. Conversational — not a letter.

P2 / P3B: Both (more common in P2). P2: invent a clear voice and details. P3B: use Data File facts; keep the blog voice the task asks for. Not a letter — no Dear… / Yours…

PAT
PurposeShare experience, opinion, or tips; engage online readers.
AudienceBlog / website readers (often teens or the public) — informal but clear.
ToneConversational, lively, first-person OK; still organised — not a chat dump.
Structure

Opening

  • Catchy title (if asked)
  • Hook (question, scene, or bold claim)
  • End with thesis / angle — last sentence of the opening paragraph

Body

  • 2–4 short paragraphs (or short headed chunks)
  • First sentence of each = topic sentence (one main idea only)
  • Then story / reason + example (after the topic sentence)
  • Personal voice + clear points

Closing

  • Sum up angle
  • Soft call to action or question to readers
  • Optional sign-off name — no formal letter ending
Skeleton

Slots: [thesis / angle] · [topic / point] · [detail / example] · [other]

Opening

[Catchy title]

[Hook]. Lately I have been thinking about [topic]. In this post, I want to share why [angle / thesis].

Body

First, [main idea 1]. Here’s why: [reason / short story]. For example, [example].

Second, [main idea 2]. If you [action], you might [result].

Finally, [tip]. Add a short reflection if it fits.

Closing

So overall, [restate angle]. What do you think — have you tried [idea]?

[Name]
Useful Phrases
  • Have you ever…? / Here’s the thing:…
  • In this post, I want to share…
  • What surprised me most was…
  • On the one hand…; on the other hand…
  • A simple tip that worked for me is…
  • Honestly, I used to think…
  • If you’re in the same boat,…
  • Drop a comment below if…
