📖 Story arc · 故事弧線 (Tap to view)
Comic mistaken-identity dialogue: a Mum scolds a boy for coming home late — but he is the neighbour’s son. Humour lands on the reveal.
喜劇認錯人：媽媽一直罵「兒子」晚歸，其實他是隔壁的男孩。笑點在結局揭曉。

Two voices: Mum = sharp, fast, rising irritation · Boy = patient, then urgent. Practise cut-offs on the Boy’s unfinished lines. · 兩種聲線：媽媽快而硬；男孩先忍後急。男孩被打斷的句子要「讀到一半就停」。

Exchange 1 · Opening
👩 Mum sharp👦 cut off
Mum And where have you been until this time? Boy I was… ✂ Mum Playing football, I bet.
Mum: crisp question — rising irritation already. Boy: start “I was…” then stop — Mum jumps in. 👀 Mum faces “son”; Boy half-raises a hand to speak.
Exchange 2 · Straight home
✂ cut-offs⚡ overlap
Boy No, I was ✂ Mum When you should have come straight home. Boy If I could explain… ✂
Boy never finishes. Mum’s “straight home” is firm — she has said this many times.
Exchange 3 · Don’t listen
👩 steamroller🔊 rising
Mum The times I tell you… ✂ Boy You see, I was… ✂ Mum To come straight home. Boy But… ✂ Mum You just don’t listen, do you? Boy I do, but… ✂ Mum In one ear and out the other. Boy But… ✂ Mum No more buts, young man.
Mum talks over him — a little faster each time. Boy’s “But…” / “I was…” stay soft and unfinished. 👀 Mum barely looks at him; Boy leans in to be heard.
Exchange 4 · Chores & tea
Boy You see… ✂ Mum Now, go and wash your hands. Boy I’m trying to tell you… ✂ Mum Tea will be on the table in five minutes. Boy The thing is…. ✂ Mum Have you any homework功課? Boy Yes, I have, but… ✂ Mum Well, after tea it’s up to your room. Boy Could I explain… ✂ Mum And no television電視 tonight.
Mum lists orders like a checklist. Keep Boy polite — still trying to speak.
Exchange 5 · Please listen
👦 urgent👩 final order
Boy I’m trying to tell you… ✂ Mum Don’t just stand there. Boy Will you listen, please? Mum Do as you’re told!
Boy’s “Will you listen, please?” is clearer and more urgent. Mum’s last order is sharp.
Exchange 6 · Mrs Wilson!!!!!
💥 breakthrough😮 pause
Boy Mrs Wilson!!!!! Mum Pardon吓？ Boy I’ve been trying to tell you! Mum What?
“Mrs Wilson!!!!!” — clear, loud energy without screaming. Tiny beat before Mum’s “Pardon?” 👀 Freeze a second after the name — then Mum’s confused face.
Exchange 7 · Reveal
😄 punchline⏱ timing
Boy That I am not your son and you’re not my mum. Mum Oh! Boy I live next door.
Punchline: clear stress on not / not. Mum’s “Oh!” = tiny shocked beat. “I live next door” — calm, then hold eye contact ~2 seconds. 👀 Smile only after “next door” lands.

💡 Listen for these contrasts:
  • Mum vs Boy: Mum is faster and interrupts; Boy’s lines often stop mid-sentence.
  • Build: Boy gets more urgent toward “Will you listen, please?”
  • Punchline: Clear “Mrs Wilson!!!!!” then a pause — then the calm reveal.
  • Ending: “I live next door” should land clearly — hold for a beat.
