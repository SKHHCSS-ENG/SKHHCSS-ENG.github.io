👀 Lazy AI vs. Smart AI Study

        
      


      

        

          
❌ Bad Way (Zero improvement in exams)

          
🧑 "Write this 400-word essay / answer Q1–Q10 for me."

          
🤖 Writes difficult C2 sentences you can't remember or use in the real DSE exam.

          
Result: Still stuck on exam day.

        


        

          
✅ Smart Way (Real DSE level-up!)

          
🧑 ① Start Chat: "Remember my passage (don't answer yet)." → ② Same Chat: "Check Q5 — give 1 line clue first!"

          
🤖 "Q5 is False vs Not Given! Check paragraph 3, line 11 first—what reason does it give?"

          
Result: Upload once · fix your own mistakes · remember it!

        

      


      

        💡 Pro Tip: Use ① Start to upload your passage or Data File once. Then stay in the same AI chat and use ② Same Chat for Q2, Q5, Q9 without taking new photos!
